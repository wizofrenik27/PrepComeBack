Talent should not depend on opportunity.

Millions of talented students have the motivation to achieve more, but many do not know where to find opportunities.

PrepComeBack aims to make discovering opportunities, learning and connecting with other students easier and more accessible.

Our mission

Build a free global ecosystem where students can discover opportunities, access resources, connect with people and create their own future.
