Talent should not depend on opportunity.

Millions of talented students around the world have the motivation to achieve more, but many simply do not know where to find opportunities.

PrepComeBack was created to make discovering opportunities, learning and connecting with other students easier and more accessible.

Our mission

Build a free global ecosystem where students can discover opportunities, access resources, connect with people and create their own future.

Built by students.

PrepComeBack is being built by students and young people who believe that education and opportunities should be more accessible.
